Vendor: Threema Work (Threema GmbH), Swiss enterprise secure messaging. Public information gathered October 2026.

Company and services: Swiss company headquartered near Zurich, about 42 employees. Threema Work is the business edition of the Threema messenger: end-to-end encrypted chat, groups, calls, file sharing, polls and central user management, as SaaS or self-hosted (Threema OnPrem, typically for 500+ licences). Published SaaS pricing: Core CHF 3.00 per user per month (up to 200 licences) and Professional CHF 5.00 per user per month (unlimited). Users can register without a phone number or email.

Security: End-to-end encryption by default with a zero-knowledge, metadata-minimising design; servers in an ISO 27001-certified data centre in Zurich. Independent audits: Cure53 audited the mobile apps (October 2020) and the new desktop app (January 2024). In January 2023 ETH Zurich researchers disclosed seven vulnerabilities in Threema's older protocol (including impersonation and key-extraction attacks); they were fixed, and Threema moved to its new "Ibex" forward-secrecy protocol, which had not been independently audited at the time of the disclosure.

Financial stability: Privately held by private equity. Afinum Management owned Threema from 2020; at the start of 2026 ownership transferred to Comitis Capital GmbH, a German investment firm, after technical due diligence by Code & Co. Revenue and profitability are not disclosed. The second private-equity sale in about five years creates some uncertainty about long-term strategy and pricing.

Regulatory compliance: Swiss data protection law plus GDPR compliance; hosting in Switzerland only (SaaS). About 2 million Threema Work users at more than 7,500 organisations, including the Swiss federal administration, the Swiss Army (the only chat app approved for army communication since January 2022), Mercedes-Benz Group, Allianz and the city of Frankfurt am Main. Note: the hosting data centre is ISO 27001 certified; a company-wide ISO 27001 certificate for Threema itself was not found in public sources.
