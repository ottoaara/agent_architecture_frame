REMOTE WORK POLICY (Synthetic example)

1. Scope
This policy applies to all permanent employees of Example Co.

2. Definitions
"Remote day" means a scheduled working day spent outside an Example Co. office.

3. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period may request up to three remote days per week.
Managers must respond to a request within ten working days.

4. Equipment
Example Co. provides a laptop. Employees must use the company VPN for all work traffic.

5. Security
Confidential documents must not be printed at home.
